I’m Jae S. Bang, an undergraduate at Seoul National University double majoring in Mathematical Sciences and Artificial Intelligence. I expect to graduate in February 2027. My current projects focus on convergence in optimization and on how to choose lens functions for Mapper.

Research interests
-----
My primary research interest lies in Optimization Theory, especially in the design and analysis of algorithms using Performance Estimation Problems(PEP) formulation, and interpreting Learning Dynamics and Model Compression in geometrical view.

Research experience
-----
### Current advisors

- [Benjamin Grimmer](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~grimmer/) (Johns Hopkins University, Fall 2026–present). I study the primal-dual gap as a measure of convergence in ρ-weakly convex optimization.
- [Woojin Kim](https://wj-kim.com/) (KAIST, Summer 2025–present). Studied persistent homology; contributed to BEAMLens as first author through a collaboration that grew out of a lab seminar.

### Previous advisors

- [Ernest K. Ryu](https://ernestryu.com/) (UCLA and OpenAI, Summer 2025–Fall 2026). I applied a sum-of-squares relaxation from the Lasserre hierarchy to avoid a nonconvex constraint in a performance estimation problem.
- [Otto van Koert](https://www.math.snu.ac.kr/~okoert/) (Seoul National University, Spring–Fall 2025). I studied homotopy types under handle attachment and intersection products in Morse theory.
- [Gye-Seon Lee](https://www.math.snu.ac.kr/~gyeseonlee/) (Seoul National University, Fall 2024–Spring 2025). I studied knots and their relation to hyperbolic structures.
